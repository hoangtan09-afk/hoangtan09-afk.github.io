Introduction: Investigate IcedID malware using VirusTotal and threat intelligence platforms to identify IOCs, associated threat actors, and execution mechanisms.

Scenario: A cyber threat group was identified for initiating widespread phishing campaigns to distribute further malicious payloads. The most frequently encountered payloads were IcedID. You have been given a hash of an IcedID sample to analyze and monitor the activities of this advanced persistent threat (APT) group.

================================================================================================

This lab introduces the IcedID malware. For those who are unfamiliar with IcedID, here is a brief summary:
- IcedID is malware designed to steal financial credentials, targeting bank accounts, cookies, and browser data.

We will collect, investigate, and synthesize information about this malware and analyze the activities that APT groups may leverage it for. We will use VirusTotal as the main platform for gathering related information.

The signatures here are rules or behaviors detected while running the sample. They are used to:
- Detect malicious behavior.
- Label activity as exploit, suspicious, malware, etc.
- Calculate a risk score.

Look deeper into the “Document exploit detected ‘UrlDownloadToFile’” section. For those who do not know what UrlDownloadToFile is:
- It means that a Word/Excel/PDF file, when executed, calls the UrlDownloadToFile API. This API is used to download files from the Internet to the machine.

Why is this marked as an exploit? Normally, Office files do not download files from the Internet on their own. If they do, it is often an exploit, macro, or second-stage payload. That is why this deserves special attention.

This signature is evidence that the .xlsm file is trying to download a file or payload from the Internet.

![pic14](/assets/img/posts/IceID/pic14.png)

The image clearly shows that Excel called the UrlDownloadToFile API, which is a sign of downloading from the Internet.

Source: EXCEL.EXE
- This behavior originates from Excel.
- This means: you open an Excel file, and Excel is being abused (via macro/exploit).

Section loaded: \KnownDlls\api-ms-win-downlevel-shlwapi-l2-1-0.dll
- This is a system DLL related to networking and file handling.
- It is used to download files.

Origin: URLDownloadToFileA
- This is the ANSI version of the download API.
- It confirms that the file is being downloaded from the Internet through HTTP/HTTPS.

The key takeaway here is that Excel is not downloading files from the Internet on its own. Something is controlling it (a macro or exploit).

Through this lab, we have practiced skills in malware analysis and threat intelligence:
- Analyzing malware samples on VirusTotal: identifying file names, hashes, extensions, history, and severity.
- Collecting IOCs: domains, URLs, and associated network behavior.
- Understanding threat actors and campaigns through MITRE ATT&CK.
- Analyzing behavior in a sandbox (Joe Sandbox): reading signatures and understanding execution flow.
- Identifying attack techniques such as T1203 Exploitation for Client Execution.
- Detecting exploit/macro indicators, such as calls to download payloads from the Internet.
